# 20 design styles: the prompts

From the @explainedbyruturaj reel "20 design styles, made in Claude". Paste any of these into Claude (with the Higgsfield connector), ChatGPT, Gemini or any image tool. Swap the subject for your own; the style words are what matter.

Every prompt ends with the same line, so the picture comes out vertical and clean:

```
Vertical 9:16. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermark.
```

## Make it move

To turn any picture into a short clip, use an image-to-video model (Kling, Wan, Seedance…) with the picture as the first frame and this prompt:

```
Bring this {style} picture to life. The camera stays completely still. {Two or three small movements: hair, water, lights, clouds}. Keep the {style} look in every frame. No text.
```
