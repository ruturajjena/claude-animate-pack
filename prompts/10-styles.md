# The 10 style prompts

Copy and paste, in order. Works in Claude with the Higgsfield connector, or in any image tool (ChatGPT, Gemini, Midjourney...).

Always redraw from the **scene** picture, never from the previous style, or your subject slowly changes.

## Step 1. Your scene

Fill in the three brackets.

```
Make a vertical 9:16 image. One subject: [who, what they wear]. One pose: [what they are doing, caught mid-action]. A simple background: [two or three things, e.g. a grassy cliff, the sea, one kite in a blue sky]. Full body in frame, subject centred, clear empty sky above. Bright, clean cartoon illustration. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermark.
```

Example: *a cheerful young man with curly black hair and round glasses in a plum hoodie* / *jumping off a skateboard with both arms up, a small corgi leaping beside him* / *a grassy cliff, the sea, a striped lighthouse, one red kite in a blue sky*.

## Step 3. Animate each picture

Attach one styled picture at a time and swap in the style name:

```
Animate this image into a 3 second clip (use the shortest length the tool allows). The camera stays completely still. Only small movements: the subject bobs gently at the top of the action, hair and clothes move a little, background details sway, clouds drift slowly. Keep the [style name] look in every frame. No text.
```

## Tips

- Simple scenes survive the redraws: one subject, one pose, a plain background, lots of sky.
- No text in the pictures. Put words in the video instead, where they stay sharp.
- If a style changes your subject, redo it once with: "Same as before, but keep the subject exactly as in the scene picture."
- Your own photo works as the scene too. Only use photos of people who said yes.
